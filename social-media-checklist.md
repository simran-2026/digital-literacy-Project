# Social Media Usage Checklist

## ✅ Do’s
- Maintain a professional profile and bio  
- Share meaningful and relevant content  
- Respect others' opinions and engage politely  
- Protect your privacy and personal information  
- Think before posting anything online  

## ❌ Don’ts
- Do not share fake or misleading information  
- Avoid posting offensive or inappropriate content  
- Do not overshare personal details  
- Avoid engaging in online arguments or negativity  
- Do not ignore privacy and security settings
